# CNRS Scientific Toolkit v0.14.1 claim boundary

Version 0.14.1 may claim improved release reproducibility, exact asset
validation, automated distribution attachment, and synchronized v0.14.0
publication metadata after those controls pass CI.

It must not claim:

- any new arithmetic capability;
- unrestricted infinite-stream arithmetic;
- a new performance advantage;
- a new Lean theorem or enlarged formal boundary;
- Lean extraction or end-to-end verification of the Python runtime;
- new physical or biological validation.

All v0.14.0 streaming-division and witness claims remain unchanged and retain
their documented exact Gaussian-rational scope.
