# Formal Scope and Open Problems

Status date: 2026-09-11

## Closed governed campaign

CNRS-LEAN-CAPSTONE closes the bounded finite-kernel campaign across six
projects. It includes finite Gaussian expansion/evaluation, exact addition and
fixed multiplication machinery, classified finite division behavior,
finite-state obstruction results, Problem 1 through P1-L7, and Problem 2
through P2-L10.

## Deliberately excluded

- arbitrary infinite-series serialization;
- ordinary-complex analytic convergence;
- analytic continuation or path reconstruction;
- unequal-branch arithmetic;
- unrestricted streaming multiplication or division;
- a canonical representation theorem for every complex number;
- physical or biological claims of the Scale Space programme.

## Software evidence boundary

Python routines remain independently implemented. Tests establish agreement
within tested domains; they are not an extraction or refinement proof from
Lean.

## Next optional campaign

No additional Lean theorem is required for this closeout. If authorized, the
next campaign is P3-L1: finite-support convolution multiplication with an
evaluation theorem. Normalization compatibility, cross-problem interfaces and
streaming research should follow as separate, explicitly bounded milestones.
